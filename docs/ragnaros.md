# Ragnaros practice

Pull freezes the roster. One human gets three Sons and one spacing body. Two humans get four Sons. Larger parties get two more Sons than humans, up to eight. The spacing body follows its route. Human health is 1,000. Ragnaros has 600 health per human. Each Son has 120 health plus 20 for each additional human.

The first surface lasts 45 seconds. The intermission activates the selected Sons and makes the same boss life unavailable for 30 seconds. A visible 1-health floor holds the boss before the return. Prevented excess damage is discarded. Defeating the boss after return and defeating every selected Son are both required. The Running deadline is 150 seconds.

The Sons have independent bodies and pursue at 1,200 mm/s. Elemental Fire gives one second of warning before 40 Fire damage, rotating through living humans near 8, 30, 86 and 108 seconds. The practice normalizes blanket Fire immunity and uses a bounded endurance check. The shorter mandatory intermission, smaller population-scaled wave, resource drain and persistence of survivors are practice choices. Historical summaries differ on blast naming and return behavior.

This Draft checkpoint supplies the roster, initial fire pressure and phase definitions. Wrath, splash, lava, passive aura, Bind and Release, and the installed observations are in progress.
