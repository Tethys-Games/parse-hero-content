# Magmadar S05 local candidate

Select `classicwow-moltencore-magmadar` and either Basic class in the compatible local
workshop product. This single encounter supports one or several humans. Right-click
Pull when everyone is ready. Key 3 recovers resource; Retry starts a fresh attempt.
Before Pull, optionally claim Fear Break and Tranquilize from their objects. These
tools are personal choices and do not occupy the solo proxy.

Magmadar opens with a Lava Bomb warning. Its floor position locks when the warning
appears; move out before the fire starts. Fire lasts ten seconds and continues after
the cast and after the boss dies. Entering it later still hurts. The objective waits
for remaining harmful fire to settle. Normal Lava Breath faces south, toward the
starting side of the room, with a finite cone warning before damage. Move out of the
warned cone; moving around the boss does not make an ordinary breath track you.

One solo spatial proxy moves once and then holds. It supplies position only and
never attacks, heals, tanks or counts toward victory. Two humans have no proxy.
Frozen participant count selects one bomb site at N=1/2, up to two at N=3/4 and up
to three at N>=5. Departure does not rescale that attempt. Sites must fit the floor,
remain separated and leave an escape corridor for each occupied warned region.

A sparse deterministic policy occasionally selects a proxy offset or a north-facing
breath. It allows zero to two selected opportunities per attempt, at least twenty
seconds apart, with no required quota. An unsafe selected placement may fall back
to an ordinary legal site and still consumes that opportunity. The published warning
always shows the committed geometry. There is no random choice in rendering.

Panic follows the fire placement. Its two-second radial warning is fixed at the
boss; leaving the six-metre radius before application avoids Fear. Every eligible
human still in range, and the declared solo proxy, receives four seconds of Fear.
Fear interrupts foreground work and compels ordinary supported movement without
choosing a safe fire route. Your class, allegiance and body assignment stay intact.
Ordinary actions and jumping are blocked; the self-only Fear Break removes one
Fear through its normal action binding. Its sixty-second personal cooldown cannot
answer each thirty-second Panic cycle. Overlapping Fear retains its own expiry.

Frenzy then grants fourteen seconds of beneficial Enrage. It doubles progress
toward routine breath opportunities, giving approximately three-second intervals
instead of six. Tranquilize removes one beneficial Enrage from the selected hostile
target; it does not cleanse Curse or harmful Magic. Removal preserves already-earned
schedule progress and the current breath's 1.8-second warning. A distant player may
legitimately avoid the breath and choose not to spend the tool. The cycle leaves a
visible response window before the first accelerated breath.

The encounter retains both Basics, one shared private Magmadar/proxy kit, a 150-second
deadline and shared Retry. It preserves the other five catalog entries. Compiler47
is an unpublished local candidate; public compiler41 and the required public gate
remain unchanged. Local validation and observations are recorded in the application
workshop runbook. This source checkpoint passes the full catalog preparer; selected
native, shipping-browser and two-client play observations remain in progress.
