# Gehennas: healing, recovery and Stun

Select `classicwow-moltencore-gehennas` with the matching unpublished compiler48
application. Both Basic Melee and Ranged use this play. The other six catalog
entries remain available. Native Melee and shipping-AOT Ranged solo victories and
local two-client healing, shared Retry and normal release are complete at the
products recorded in the application workshop runbook. Independent access to the
original local observations remains pending; source/CI acceptance is separate.

Right-click the Remove Curse and Field Recovery pedestals to opt in, then Pull.
Field Recovery casts for 1.5 seconds and restores up to 400 health to the exact
living selected ally or yourself. It has two uses per holder per attempt and a
12-second entitlement cooldown. Full health and a suppressed heal still spend a
use. An interrupted cast or invalid recipient does not. The HUD shows the current
preview and the amount actually delivered. Retry starts the next attempt, where
tools must be claimed again.

Gehennas Curse reduces incoming healing to 25% for 15 seconds. Remove Curse removes
one harmful Curse from a friendly target or yourself; its eight-second cooldown
allows distributed service. Cleansing when no healing is needed and leaving a
recovery unused are valid choices. Movement, cleansing and recovery remain usable
during settlement when otherwise eligible.

Kill Gehennas and every selected guard. Solo has one required guard and one
optional spatial proxy. Two or more humans have two required guards and no proxy.
The proxy has no actions. A guard warns for 1.3 seconds before a four-metre Fist
applies two seconds of Stun to the complete eligible cohort still in range.
Leave the warning early; Stun interrupts foreground work and blocks movement and
actions, including Fear Break. Existing independent deliveries continue.

The boss uses one finite mode program: Rain, cycling Curse, then cycling exact
Shadow Bolts. Rain locks its sites during a two-second warning and persists for
eight seconds. Site count is `min(3, ceil(N / 2))`, with separated sites and
supported escape paths. The nominal cycle is 30 seconds. Bolt pressure is 100
damage per accepted delivery, with six-second opportunities in its finite mode.
The boss starts at 1,250 health, adding 500 per additional frozen human. These
values retain room for both Basic classes' recovery and movement decisions.
Already committed Rain and Stun survive source death until their finite end.

The frozen roster supports 100 humans numerically; this does not promise 100
simultaneous copies of every tool. Both Basics, boss and guard use four kits. The
shared 128-entitlement, per-body slot, effect, timer and snapshot bounds still
apply before admission. The three-site Rain cap does not reduce its contact cohort.

This is a compressed practice adaptation of Gehennas: solo guard count, short
stuns, finite personal recovery, no tank/aggro assignment and no sustained healer
role. The declared deadline remains 150 seconds; health and pressure are tuning
values. The original retained Magmadar observations keep their original content
producer. Public compiler41, trusted validator, main and provider execution are
unchanged.
