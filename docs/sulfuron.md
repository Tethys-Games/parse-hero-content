Choose Melee or Ranged, then right-click the Interrupt pedestal and optionally
Cleanse Magic before Pull. Interrupt reaches 2.5 metres and has a six-second
cooldown. Cleanse Magic reaches a friendly actor or yourself within twelve metres
and removes one harmful Magic occurrence, with an eight-second cooldown. Tools are
personal opt-ins; claiming fewer tools does not reduce the critical-cast challenge.

Pull freezes the human count. One human faces two priests, two face three, and
three or more face four. Sulfuron and every selected priest must die. Each priest
uses one finite controller for Dark Mending, Immolate, Shadow Word Pain, routine
weapon pressure and its route. Dark Mending chooses the living wounded ally with
the lowest health fraction, including the caster. Its 2.5-second cast heals 175
actual health, capped at the recipient's frozen maximum. Interrupting an incidental
spell can leave the next heal uncovered; a completed heal costs time, not a hidden
failure flag.

The Dark Mending group has one, two or three coverage slots according to frozen
N. Each slot retains eight seconds of spacing after an accepted launch, including
an interrupted launch. A priest has one finite fourteen-second opportunity window;
missed windows do not accumulate a queue. Coordinate which visible priest each
participant covers. The ordinary cast and health displays show the result.

Blue paths and tether rings show the finite routes. The yellow 4.5-metre circle
around the living Sulfuron marks the nearby-ally cadence relationship: a priest
inside it earns routine attack progress at 1.75 times normal speed. Leaving the
circle or defeating Sulfuron ends new accelerated progress. It never speeds up
Dark Mending or erases progress already earned. Priests cross this boundary through
real movement; their health does not reset at the tether.

Hand of Ragnaros warns for 2.2 seconds in a five-metre captured circle, then applies
40 damage, 1.5 seconds of Stun and an outward planar impulse to the complete current
eligible cohort. Escape during the warning or recover range after displacement.
Flame Spear tracks one exact target for 1.2 seconds of its two-second cast, then
holds the ground location. Every player still in the two-metre splash at impact
takes 35 damage; separate or leave the circle.

Demoralizing Shout warns for one second and reduces weapon-action source potency
to 65% for eight seconds. Melee Attack and Cleave are weapon actions; Ranged Attack
is an Arcane spell, independently of its seeking delivery. Classification comes
from the definitions, not class names or distance. Immolate and Shadow Word Pain
are finite harmful Magic periodic effects and can be cleansed.
