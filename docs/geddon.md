# Baron Geddon practice

The compiler57 Draft entry is `classicwow-moltencore-geddon`. This first S14 checkpoint covers the frozen roster, Ignite and the low-health finish. Living Bomb and Inferno are still in development.

Pull freezes the enrolled humans. Geddon has 600 health per enrolled human. Humans and the two optional route-following bodies have 1,000 health apiece. One human gets two spacing bodies, two humans get one, and three or more humans get none. Each spacing body can move and be hurt. Its route supplies no combat actions. A defeated body stays defeated for the attempt.

Ignite selects one enrolled human at each opportunity, starting at 22 seconds and repeating every 24 seconds. Its single harmful Magic effect lasts 12 seconds and pulses every two seconds, including the expiration boundary. Each pulse drains up to 10 of the recipient?s current class resource, then deals 20 base Fire damage. Damage mitigation and immunity apply to the health output. Taking Dispel Magic lets the party remove the occurrence and all its remaining pulses. Field Recovery is a separately acquired tool with two uses per attempt.

Original Ignite Mana tied damage to the mana actually burned. This practice uses a class-neutral resource and deliberately deals the declared damage even when the recipient has zero resource. The practice also normalizes the original Fire immunity for class access.

The first hit that leaves a living Geddon at or below 10% starts one eight-second finish countdown. The party must defeat him before its expiration cut. A lethal hit from above the threshold can finish immediately. The ordinary Running deadline is 120 seconds. Pending finite harm still has to settle, and the death of the last enrolled human defeats the party.
