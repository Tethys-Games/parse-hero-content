health, kit, combat or utility actions. Parties get no proxy. Remove Curse remains the

deadline. Visible nondispellable Practice Durability applies 400-permille incoming

reference, a finite supported body-clear destination and world evidence. Admission

retarget or manufacture a ground plane. Pending/Accepted gates new offense. Only the
exact Applied result starts two seconds of recovery, followed by the next full warning.
Reference death before commitment rejects; death afterward cannot recall the move.

nominal six-second pulse schedule outside the other finite modes. Counterspell has a

and following each completed Blink recovery. It interrupts the exact incumbent casts
captured on its due cut. Empty sectors are valid. Prior paid installments and committed
independent flights/displacements survive; an aborted batch has no prefix interruption
or new final payment. It has no damage, school silence or automatic retry.

The frontal geometry is provisional: source descriptions disagree about area versus
frontal coverage. Exact-cast interruption also omits the source game's school lockout.
Both are deliberate practice adaptations; the source conflict remains unresolved.
