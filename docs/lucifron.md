# Lucifron S02 utility candidate

Select `classicwow-moltencore-lucifron` from `workspace.json`. Both public Basic roots
belong to this one Session. This source is a local candidate; installation and play
evidence belong to application PR527. Harmful control and rescue are selected next in
S03 and are not implemented by this S02 content.

Choose a class in Preparation. Right-click either utility pedestal to take Remove Curse
on key 5 or Dispel Magic on key 6. Solo can take both; a party can divide the tools or
take redundant coverage. Both have an eight-second cooldown and cost zero. Select
yourself or a base ally within 12 metres before cleansing. Remove Curse removes one
oldest harmful Curse; Dispel removes one oldest harmful Magic occurrence. Missing
tools do not block Pull and do not receive automatic replacement coverage.

Pull freezes actual human membership and admits Lucifron plus exactly two Protectors.
Solo also gets one allied DPS proxy. Every selected body must finish installation before
combat and the two-minute deadline start together. Lucifron starts at 1,600 health plus
600 for each additional frozen human; each Protector starts at 450 plus 400. The proxy
has 1,000 health, deals 20 damage per two-second strike and uses ordinary paid attacks
and free resource recovery. It supplies no utility or healing, is not a human life or
required objective, and never appears as a replacement for a dead/departed teammate.

Lucifron holds position. Cost Curse first becomes available near six seconds and
doubles the selected human's resource costs for twelve seconds; its cooldown is
24 seconds. Doom first becomes available near fourteen seconds, cycles one eligible
base-party recipient at a time and deals one 600-damage pulse eight seconds later.
An already affected recipient is excluded. Curse and Doom require real range;
staying out of range does not cause a substitute application. Protectors approach
humans and use staggered, visible frontal cleaves. Close Shadow Shock has a visible
tell and six-second cooldown. Complete area queries reject overflow rather than clipping.

Basic key 3 recovers 40 resource every six seconds, even from zero. Melee Attack and
Cleave cost 8 and 16; Ranged Attack and Basic Curse cost 12 each. Basic Curse remains
the original harmful Magic DoT, distinct from Lucifron's cost Curse. Cleanse changes
future costs; accepted casts keep their original quote and paid installments.

Defeat all three required enemies in any order. A timely final kill records the objective,
then Settling keeps already applied Doom and other mandatory harm real after source death.
Remove the effect or survive it. Harmless timers do not delay victory; all human lives
defeated still fails, including the same cut as the last enemy. A timely objective stays
timely when settlement ends after the deadline. Movement, removals and recovery remain
usable. Party Retry settles and fences the old attempt before fresh Preparation; Leave
uses normal teardown. Cadences and health are provisional until the selected product
observations establish useful roughly 90–120-second play. No mandatory cycle count gates victory.
