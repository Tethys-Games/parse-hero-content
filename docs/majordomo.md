# Majordomo Executus practice

Choose `classicwow-moltencore-majordomo` with Basic Melee or Basic Ranged.
This partial S12 content checkpoint adds the eleventh play for candidate compiler54.
The roster, casts, optional Interrupt, personal protection, fixed coals and two recipient relocations are authored. Polymorph, its halfway transition and the S13 shields are still in development. Installed solo and party observations are pending.

| Classic encounter | Practice decision |
| --- | --- |
| Four Elites and four Healers guard Executus | Pull freezes 2+2 adds for one human, 3+3 for two, and 4+4 for three through 100. Defeating the selected adds is the objective. Executus carries personal Aegis. |
| Healers can be controlled until four guardians die | S12 calls for halfway at 2, 3 or 4 genuine deaths, release of surviving controlled Healers and damage-breaking Polymorph. This checkpoint contains the damaging casts. |
| Elite Fire and Healer Shadow pressure | The shared Flamewaker kit assigns Fire Blast and Blast Wave to Elites and Shadow Bolt to Healers. Fixed cadences and staggered opportunities cap dangerous casts at one per human, up to three. The Healer has no heal spell. |
| Teleport into the burning coals | Any eligible frozen human can be captured in cyclic order. Two finite cast windows target the same supported coal anchor. Walking out limits subsequent damage from the field. |
| Executus survives the surrender | His own 60-second, nondispellable protection has two finite renewal windows. Adds receive no Aegis. Committed harm must finish before a victory. |
| Damage Shield and a 50% spell reflection chance | The selected S13 design uses deterministic alternating windows. Physical-family retaliation includes ranged physical hits. Initial harmful spells can reflect once. Those shield mechanics are pending. |
| Raid organization, threat, healing and summoning | Both paths retain the ordinary Basics. There is no solo proxy, automatic control or healing, or full raid logistics. |

The starting practice profile gives each human 1,000 health. Each selected add has 350 health plus 150 for each additional frozen human. Executus has 1,000 health. The attempt deadline is 150 seconds from actual combat start. The ordinary Recover action restores the Basic's resource.
