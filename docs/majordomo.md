# Majordomo Executus practice

Choose `classicwow-moltencore-majordomo` with Basic Melee or Basic Ranged.
This Majordomo candidate is the eleventh play for compiler56. The installed observations below used its S12 predecessor. At preparation, take Polymorph, Interrupt or both. To control a Healer, select its body and complete the 0.8-second Polymorph cast within 12 m. A successful cast costs no resource, has no cooldown and holds that Healer for up to 20 seconds. A successful refresh restarts the clock. Positive damage breaks the hold, so direct attacks and incidental area damage both matter. Both installed solo paths have defeated all four adds and reached surrender. Basic Melee finished in 84.07 seconds and shipping-AOT Basic Ranged in 95.13 seconds. Each completed Retry and ordinary release. The first shipping-AOT Ranged attempt ended in acquisition before combat and remains an open incident. In the automated local party, Basic Melee interrupted Shadow Bolt and Basic Ranged held a Healer with Polymorph. Ranged kept fighting after Melee died in the coals. Both received the shared loss, retried and exited normally. Human multiplayer feel remains unqualified.
